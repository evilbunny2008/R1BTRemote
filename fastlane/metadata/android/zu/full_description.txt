Shintsha i-remote eshibhile ye-Bluetooth ye-Beauty-R1 ibe yi-remote yemidiya ye-Android.

Ngokuzenzakalelayo i-Beauty-R1 izenza isikrini sokuthinta: konke ukucindezela inkinobho kuwukuswayipha noma ukuthepha okungelona iqiniso, ngakho i-Android ibonisa ikhesa yegundane futhi ukucindezela kuwela kunoma yini engaphansi kwayo. I-R1 BT Remote ibamba lokho kucindezela futhi ikushintshe kube izilawuli zemidiya, ukuze ukwazi ukudlala, ukumisa isikhashana, ukweqa amathrekhi nokushintsha ivolumu.

OKWENZIWA YIZINKINOBHO

• Phakathi: dlala / misa isikhashana
• Kwesokudla: ithrekhi elandelayo
• Kwesokunxele: ithrekhi edlule
• Bamba kwesokudla: shesha uye phambili
• Bamba kwesokunxele: buyisela emuva
• Phezulu: khulisa ivolumu
• Phansi: nciphisa ivolumu

Izilawuli zisebenza nanoma iyiphi i-app esabela ezinkinobhweni ezijwayelekile zemidiya, njengezidlali zomculo, ama-app ama-podcast nezidlali zezincwadi ezilalelwayo.

INDLELA ESEBENZA NGAYO

• Isevisi yokubamba nge-Shizuku (iyanconywa). Uma i-Shizuku isebenza, i-app iqala isevisi encane enamalungelo e-shell ethatha ukulawula okukhethekile kwedivayisi yokufaka ye-remote. I-Android ayikuboni nhlobo ukucindezela, ngakho ayikho ikhesa evelayo. Uma i-remote inqamuka, isevisi iyayilinda futhi iyithathe futhi uma ixhuma kabusha.

• Okunye ngokufinyeleleka. Uma i-Shizuku ingatholakali, isevisi yokufinyeleleka ibamba imicimbi yesikhombi ye-remote futhi iyishintshe ibe yizenzo zemidiya. Lokhu kusebenza ngaphandle kwe-Shizuku, kodwa i-Android isabonisa ikhesa esikrinini.

Isikrini esikhulu sibonisa isimo sakho kokubili, futhi sibonisa kuphela izinkinobho zokusetha zezinyathelo ongakazenzi.

IZIDINGO

• I-Android 14 noma entsha
• I-remote ye-Bluetooth ye-Beauty-R1 (ivela njengo-"Beauty-R1")
• Akuphoqelekile kodwa kuyanconywa: i-Shizuku

ISEVISI YOKUFINYELELEKA

Isevisi yokufinyeleleka isetshenziselwa kuphela ukufunda okufakwayo kusuka ku-remote ye-Beauty-R1 nokukushintsha kube ukucindezela kokhiye bemidiya. Ayifundi okuqukethwe kwesikrini, futhi i-app ayiqoqi noma ithumele noma yiluphi ulwazi.
